## Newer construction, acreage and one-level living

Built in 2021, this home gives you the space of country living without giving up a practical floor plan. You get the acreage and the quiet, but the house itself is newer, efficient and easy to live in.

The kitchen anchors the main living area with white cabinetry, tall pantry storage and a large center island — the kind of island people actually end up standing around. The dining area is open to it, so the whole main living space works as one room.

All three bedrooms, both bathrooms and the laundry room are on the same level. The primary suite is set apart from the other bedrooms and includes a walk-in closet, a private bath with a soaking tub, a separate shower and a double vanity.

## The land

The property sits on 10.23 acres with a cleared yard surrounded by trees. There is a storage shed plus an open-front shed, which covers the equipment, tools and toys that usually end up taking over a garage. A large gravel parking area handles extra vehicles, trailers or a camper.

Access is off paved County Road 19, so you are not fighting a long dirt road every spring.

## Who this home fits

If you have been looking in the Deer River and Grand Rapids area for acreage that does not come with a 1970s fixer-upper attached, this is a rare combination. Newer homes on ten-plus acres in Itasca County do not come up often, and when they do they usually carry a higher price than this one.
