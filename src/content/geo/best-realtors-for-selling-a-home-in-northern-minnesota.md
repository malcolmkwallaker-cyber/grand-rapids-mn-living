## What Should Sellers Know About Timing a Listing?

Spring and summer bring the most buyer activity in northern Minnesota, particularly for lakefront and cabin properties that buyers want to see and use before the season ends. Listing in April or May, ahead of peak inventory, generally gives a seller the best combination of buyer attention and less competition from other listings.

Winter listings move slower but attract more serious, motivated buyers who aren't just browsing. Malcolm and Bridger walk sellers through the actual tradeoffs of listing in each season rather than defaulting to "list in spring" as a one-size-fits-all answer, since a seller's personal timeline sometimes outweighs the seasonal advantage.

## What Do Sellers Need to Know About Well, Septic, and Rural Disclosures?

Most homes outside city limits in northern Minnesota run on private well and septic systems, and Minnesota requires specific disclosures around septic system compliance before a sale can close in many cases. Sellers who skip this step, or who don't know it applies to them, can end up with a deal that falls apart during inspection.

Malcolm and Bridger walk sellers through what needs testing, what needs a compliance inspection, and what a buyer's lender will require before closing. This is routine work for them and unfamiliar territory for an agent who mostly sells suburban homes on city water and sewer.
